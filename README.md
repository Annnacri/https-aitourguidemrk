## ✨ From idea to itinerary in minutes

Creating a professional tour or travel itinerary traditionally means researching destinations, organizing activities, writing descriptions, structuring schedules and formatting everything for the customer.

AI Tourguide Creator simplifies that workflow.

Describe the experience you want to create — and let AI help transform the idea into a structured, customer-ready travel experience.

### Example

> *"Create a 3-day experience in Lisbon for a French couple, combining gastronomy, culture and a romantic experience."*

The platform can transform that request into a structured travel experience with:

* 🗺️ Personalized itineraries
* 🍷 Gastronomy and local experiences
* 🏛️ Culture and attractions
* ❤️ Romantic and special-interest experiences
* 🚶 Tours and activities
* 🌍 Multilingual content
* 📄 Professional travel materials
* 🤖 AI-assisted travel planning
